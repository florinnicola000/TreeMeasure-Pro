Features

Everything you need in the field.

Professional tools for forest inventory, reporting and GIS, right on your iPhone.

📏

LiDAR Diameter

Depth-assisted DBH measurement with a manual reticle available for field checks and calibration.

📐

AR Height

Real-time augmented clinometer. Calculates height using trigonometry and motion sensors.

🌳

Sample Plot

Standard inventory with configurable radius. Auto-calculates basal area (G), N/ha, and total volume.

🔭

Bitterlich Method

Integrated digital relascope with configurable BAF for fast basal area estimates from the viewfinder.

🗺️

GPS Map

Every record geolocated and shown on an interactive map. Filter by measurement type.

🧮

Volume & Coefficients

Built-in form factor table for 124 species. Select species, the app calculates volume automatically.

📊

PDF · CSV · GeoJSON

PDF reports, spreadsheet-ready tables and GeoJSON data for GIS/QField workflows.

📡

Offline Field Work

Records stay local on device and can be collected in the field without an active connection.

🌿

124 Forest Species

Native species with autocomplete, scientific names, and species-specific volume coefficients.

🌐

Crown Measurement

Crown diameter and base height via AR. Automatic crown-to-trunk ratio and surface area calculation.

📉

Clinometer / Slope

Measure forest road slope in degrees and percentage. Auto-classification per Romanian forestry standards.

📏

Circumference

Measure trees too wide for calipers — walk around the trunk, the app fits a circle to bark-surface points. Works on any iPhone, no LiDAR needed.

🪵

Round Log

Log volume using Smalian or Huber formulas. Diameters at both ends and midpoint, 3D length and volume calculated automatically.

⌚

Apple Watch

Trigger measurements from your wrist with Double Tap or a wrist twist — both hands free to aim at the tree.

How it works

Simple from the very first tree.

From opening the app to exporting your data — in four steps.

1

Choose a method

Select your workflow: full inventory, AR height, crown, sample plot, or Bitterlich.

2

Point the camera

Aim at the tree. AR guides help you position the phone correctly for optimal results.

3

Measure

AR guidance, LiDAR and motion sensors help calculate field values.

4

Save & export

Records are saved with GPS and timestamp. Export PDF, CSV or GeoJSON.
